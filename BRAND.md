# ORVEX — Brand foundation

## Official positioning
**FR:** Tout votre business. Un seul espace.  
**EN:** Your Entire Business. One Space.

ORVEX is an independent premium technology brand. It is not a sub-brand of Codecontenu.

## Official languages
French and English are the two official communication languages. Product interface, onboarding, transactional emails, help content and public website should support both. Users must be able to change language at any time.

## Visual direction
- Premium, modern, confident and minimal.
- Midnight navy: `#101827`
- Soft ivory: `#F4F3EF`
- Premium teal: `#32CDBB`
- Silver grey: `#A9B1BE`
- Electric blue may be used as a restrained accent; final token values are pending approval.

## Design principles
- Strong contrast and accessible typography.
- Clear hierarchy; avoid visual clutter.
- Consistent spacing, rounded surfaces and restrained gradients.
- Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop.
- Do not finalize a logo or claim trademark/domain availability until separately reviewed.
